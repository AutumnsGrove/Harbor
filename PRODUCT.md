# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person: Autumn, saving things from her phone and laptop through the day (links, notes, images,
quotes), then coming back to find them. She opens Harbor on the tailnet, usually at a desk in the
evening or on her phone in short bursts. She replaced Raindrop and mymind with it, so she knows what
the good ones feel like. There is no second user, no sharing and no onboarding audience.

## Product Purpose

A personal save-anything library that files itself. The product is the save gesture; the library only
has to be trustworthy and findable. Success is that saving feels like Cmd+D, nothing is ever lost or
shown as failed, and opening the library feels like opening your own shelf rather than an admin tool.

## Brand Personality

Calm, owned, a little nautical. A harbour at dusk: dark water, a few lights, boats tied up safely.
Quiet confidence over cleverness. Warm toward the user's things; the interface recedes so saved covers
carry the colour.

## Anti-references

- Raindrop's utilitarian bookmark-manager look.
- Generic dark-mode SaaS dashboards (blue-grey cards, a purple gradient, hero metrics).
- Pinterest-style noise: busy chrome competing with the content.
- CNCF Harbor, the container registry (the name collision is known; the look must not echo it).
- Anything that treats a dead link or a paywall as an error. Those are badges, not alarms.

## Design Principles

1. **The save never fails, and the UI never says it did.** Pending, walled and dead are first-class,
   calm states, never red alarms.
2. **The content is the colour.** Chrome stays quiet so covers and kinds carry the page.
3. **Safe harbour, not a control room.** Warmth and a sense of place beat density and instruments.
4. **One gesture, one answer.** Every screen has one obvious thing to do; everything else waits on hover.
5. **Earned familiarity.** Standard patterns for standard tasks; personality goes into the brand
   moments (wordmark, empty states, saving, arriving), not into form controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast minimum on the dark surface. Every control keyboard reachable with a visible focus
ring. Motion stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Colour never carries meaning alone: amber, magenta
and teal states always have a label or icon too.
