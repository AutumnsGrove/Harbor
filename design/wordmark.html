Harbor, three ways to sign it

Each lockup is shown large, at nav-bar size beside the search field, and as the favicon at 16, 32 and 48 px. All three keep the teal-black ground; the light is warm because a harbour at dusk is lit, not neon.

A

Lantern

A harbour light: an arched lantern with a lit core. The most literal and the most mark-like, it holds up as an app icon and gives the empty states a character to use.

The o is the light

No icon. The second o in "harbor" becomes a ring with a lit core, so the name carries the whole idea. Quietest of the three; favicon is the ring alone.

Mooring H

Two quay posts and a line slung between them make the H. It reads as a letter first and a harbour second, and the magenta rope keeps the brand's "act" colour in the mark.
